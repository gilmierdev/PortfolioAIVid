How I build a winning ad

The same structure behind every spec ad on this site — no more than 30 seconds, five moves.

00:00

Hook

The first few seconds earn the rest of the watch — a specific moment, not a generic opener.

00:03

Problem

Name the problem the buyer already recognizes, and let it sit before the product shows up.

00:08

Mechanism

Explain why this product works when whatever they tried before didn't.

00:16

Proof

Reviews, results or a demo — something that makes the claim believable, not just louder.

00:24

Call to action

One clear next step. Not three options — one.

Process

What happens after a brief lands, in order.

  1. 01

    Understand the brief

    Read it fully first. Ask about anything unclear, and confirm the brand, the ICP and any must-follow rules before starting.

  2. 02

    Find a reference

    Look at what's already winning in the category — the hook, structure, pacing and angle — without copying it.

  3. 03

    Concept and script

    Write the hook-to-CTA structure as a short script or storyboard, and send it for approval before generating anything.

  4. 04

    Generate, animate, voice

    Iterate the approved concept into an original first frame, animate it, then voice it.

  5. 05

    Edit and deliver a draft

    Cut, caption and mix, then send a draft rather than a finished file.

  6. 06

    Revise

    Take feedback and revise — I follow direction rather than go rogue on an approved concept.
